What's App

WhatsApp allows users to send text messages, voice messages, images, videos, documents, and even make voice and video calls over the internet. Instagram

Instagram is a social media platform also owned by Meta Platforms. Launched in 2010, it is primarily focused on photo and video sharing. Users can upload images, short videos (Reels), and share their moments via Stories, which disappear after 24 hours. It also allows direct messaging, live streaming, and shopping through the app. Instagram has become a major platform for influencers, brands, and creators

Snapchat

Snapchat is a multimedia messaging app developed by Snap Inc., launched in 2011. Its main feature is the ability to send "Snaps" – photos or videos that disappear after being viewed. Snapchat introduced the concept of Stories, where users can post content visible to friends for 24 hours. It also includes features like filters, lenses (AR effects), Bitmojis, and streaks.

YouTube

YouTube is the world’s largest video-sharing platform, launched in 2005 and now owned by Google. It allows users to upload, view, share, and comment on videos across a wide variety of topics such as entertainment, education, music, vlogs, tutorials, and more.
